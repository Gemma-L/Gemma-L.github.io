01 / RESEARCH

People at the centre
of the conversation.

My research connects communication, user research, and human–AI interaction. I investigate how everyday understandings of AI shape what people expect from it—and what they do with it.

I am completing my PhD at NTU under the supervision of Professor Chen Lou. Before my PhD, I studied Digital Humanities at King’s College London and worked in product management, alongside projects in user research and design.
